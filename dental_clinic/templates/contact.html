Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your ID, insurance card (if applicable), and any previous dental records. Also, arrive 15 minutes early to complete new patient forms.

Do you accept dental insurance?

Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans. Please contact us with your specific insurance information for detailed coverage information.

How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend visiting the dentist every 6 months for regular checkups and cleanings. However, some patients may need more frequent visits based on their oral health.

What if I have a dental emergency?

For dental emergencies, please call us immediately at 6304853230 or 8309718235. We provide emergency dental care during our clinic hours (10:00 AM - 10:00 PM).
